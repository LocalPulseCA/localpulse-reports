3 things we found about GlowUp Medispa

1

You have replied to 49 of your 185 Google reviews. Sublime MediSpa, 4.9 km from you, has replied to 129 of its 132. A short thank-you under each review shows new clients that someone is paying attention, and Google lists replying to reviews as a way to improve local ranking.

2

Clients cannot book a time with you online. Your website sends them to a contact form instead. 14 of the 17 clinics within 10 km of you let clients pick a time and book online, including the two closest, Sadaat (4.7 km) and Sublime MediSpa (4.9 km). Every booking waits on someone from your team to follow up, while a client comparing you with Sadaat at 10 pm can book with them on the spot.

3

44 of the 76 written 4- and 5-star Google reviews that name someone on your team name you (58%). The next most named, Gabby, is in 18. Your website and booking page list 6 people who treat clients, so an even split would be about 17% each. Clients are booking you personally, and that ties how far the clinic can grow to your own calendar.
